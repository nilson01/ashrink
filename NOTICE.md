# Attribution and license

Adaptive shrinkage was introduced by Matthew Stephens, “False discovery rates:
a new deal,” Biostatistics 18(2), 275–294 (2017), doi:10.1093/biostatistics/kxw041.
R ashr is maintained by Peter Carbonetto and its credited authors/contributors;
its source and documentation informed the numerical contracts. Multivariate
shrinkage follows Urbut et al. (2019), doi:10.1038/s41588-018-0268-8, with R mashr
as a numerical reference. Gaussian regression follows Kim, Wang, Carbonetto
and Stephens (2024), JMLR 25(185), https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/22-0953.html;
mr.ash.alpha is a reference implementation. Logistic variational fitting uses
the bound of Jaakkola and Jordan (1997), PMLR R1:283–294,
https://proceedings.mlr.press/r1/jaakkola97a.html. Existing Python empirical-Bayes software includes ebnmpy.
